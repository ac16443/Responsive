## Bootstrap break points

*Extra small --> < 576px (Mini Phone)  
*Small --> >=576px (Regular Phone) sm
*Extra small --> >=768px (Tablets)  md
*Extra small --> >=992px (Laptops)   lg
*Extra small --> >=1200px (Monitors )  xl
*Extra small --> >=1400px (Monitors and Bigger screens )  xxl
